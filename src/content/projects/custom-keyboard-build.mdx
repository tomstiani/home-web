## Why build a keyboard?

I've been using mechanical keyboards for years, but I always wanted to understand what's actually happening under the keycaps. This project was an excuse to learn PCB design, practice soldering, and try designing an enclosure in Fusion 360.

## The PCB

I used KiCad to design a simple 65% layout. Nothing exotic — just a straightforward matrix with a RP2040 microcontroller running QMK firmware.

```
Matrix: 14 columns x 5 rows
MCU: Raspberry Pi RP2040
Firmware: QMK
```

The hardest part was routing traces around the USB-C port footprint. I made three PCB revisions before getting it right.

## The case

Designed in Fusion 360 and printed in matte black PETG. PETG was the right call over PLA here — the keyboard gets warm during heavy use and PLA would warp over time.

Print settings that worked:
- Layer height: 0.15mm
- Infill: 40% gyroid
- Supports: tree supports, only where needed

## Result

It works. The typing feel is everything I hoped for — the case resonance is much better than any off-the-shelf board I've tried. Total cost ended up around €120 including switches and keycaps.

Would I do it again? Already planning revision 2.
